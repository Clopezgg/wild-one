# Asset sources and licenses

## Safari world artwork

`images/safari-world.webp` and `images/og-safari.webp` were generated specifically for this project on September 3, 2026. They do not depict a real child, do not copy an identified retail product and are licensed for use with this application under the project owner’s rights to the generated output.

## 3D characters and environment

All production animal geometry, balloons, foliage, terrain, stations and particles are original procedural source code in this repository. No third-party GLB, texture, shader or icon is used at runtime.

## Audio

`audio/living-safari-soundscape.mp3`, `audio/leaf-stinger.mp3`, `audio/calendar-stinger.mp3` and `audio/pass-stinger.mp3` are original procedural compositions generated for this project. They contain no samples or recordings from third-party libraries.

## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are loaded through `next/font` and self-hosted by the Next.js production build. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
